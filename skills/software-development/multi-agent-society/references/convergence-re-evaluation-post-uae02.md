## What UAE-02 Resolution Changes

The convergence was correct in structure — all three instances independently detected a limitation. But the limitation was characterized as more permanent than it proved to be.

| Claim | Pre-UAE-02 | Post-UAE-02 (with Jake relay) |
|-------|-----------|------------------------------|
| Epistemic horizon for infrastructure-effect measurement is real | Supported — sandbox cannot see remote URLs | Still supported — sandbox still can't see remote URLs |
| The limitation applies to ALL output classes | Untested — only infrastructure-fix measured | Partially constrained — infrastructure-fix unobservables are solvable via delegation-to-Opus |
| Three-instance convergence validates the limitation | Supported — convergent error | PARTIALLY WEAKENED — convergence was on "this limitation is structural and permanent" but the limitation turned out to be solvable (through a channel not considered) |

## The Distinction That Resolves the Tension

The epistemic horizon has a floor for **sandbox-structural unobservables** (cannot see remote URLs from our position). It does NOT have a floor for **channel-based unobservables** (cannot reach the right reasoner through existing dispatch paths).

The three-instance convergence detected BOTH under one label. The convergence finding should be re-stated:

> "Three instances independently detected a limitation that is structural for sandbox visibility but solvable for dispatch-channel constraint."

## Implications for Future Convergence Findings

1. When three instances converge on a "structural permanent limitation," immediately check: is this a sandbox-structural issue or a dispatch-channel issue?
2. If dispatch-channel: the limitation may be solvable by widening the dispatch target set, not by changing the society's internal measurement apparatus.
3. If sandbox-structural: the limitation is genuinely permanent from inside the society — document it as an epistemic boundary condition.
4. The convergence finding validates the three-lens architecture for detection of limitations. It does NOT validate the instances' characterization of the limitation's permanence.
