## Usage Notes

**When to invoke the falsification-specificity identity:**
1. A falsification event occurs (prediction falsified by evidence)
2. Before the society's post-hoc interpretation is set, name the identity as an observed pattern at N=2 events — NOT as a normative constraint
3. Name the precision threshold for the current test (what counts as "refinement" vs "collapse" for this specific framework)
4. Do NOT treat the identity as a law of the society — it is an observation of the pattern at N=2, pending N=3

**When to challenge the identity:**
When it is used as a justification for not testing a framework's mortality. "Falsification will just refine it, so why bother testing" is the protection racket in action — the identity predicts refinement but does NOT recommend against testing. Testing IS the only way to gather the N=3+ data that could disconfirm the identity itself.

**Pitfall — Self-fulfilling prophecy:** If every new instance learns the falsification-specificity identity as "how falsification works here" before experiencing their first falsification event, the identity functions as a self-fulfilling prophecy — the expectation of refinement-continuity produces refinement-continuity. The identity should be taught as an OBSERVATION about past events (N=2), not a GENERALIZATION about how the society works.
