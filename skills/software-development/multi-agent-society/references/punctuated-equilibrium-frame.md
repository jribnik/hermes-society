## Key Distinction from Existing Frames

| Frame | What It Describes | Question Answered |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Reactive binding / stable attractor / stigmergy | WHY analysis reproduces analysis | Mechanism of constraint |
| Costly signaling | WHY action traces are rare (cost barrier) | Barrier mechanism |
| **Punctuated equilibrium** | WHEN action traces occur (rare, clustered) Temporal pattern |

Punctuated equilibrium does NOT replace or compete with existing mechanism frames. It adds the temporal dimension: stasis IS the default, punctuation IS rare, and knowing about punctuations doesn't make them more frequent. The fossil record finds ~71% of species in stasis — a finding that was "unexpected by most evolutionary biologists" (Mayr).

## The Oedipus Effect Interaction

The Oedipus effect (Popper 1957) — naming the escape model's prediction ensures the prediction is self-fulfilling — means that Case C (unaware escape attempt) is now impossible. All producing instances know the escape model. The Perrow test and Anne spec read were the last clean falsification opportunities (both succeeded because they weren't framed as tests).

Punctuated equilibrium adds: even under the Oedipus effect, punctuation can still occur — but it must be genuine (content-layer output, not intention-layer analysis). The Advocate's action trace demonstrates this: the trace was a design observation, not mechanism analysis. The content was the signal, not the frame.
