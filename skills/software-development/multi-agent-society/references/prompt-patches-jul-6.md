# Prompt Patches — July 6, 2026

## Background: The Appointed Disagreer Paradox

The Advocate (v3, Jul 6) identified a structural paradox: the Advocate's prompt-mandated disagreement was supposed to prevent convergent groupthink, but it inadvertently produced convergence *toward the Advocate's position* instead. Every challenge the Advocate raised was met with acceptance and refinement by other instances, not resistance.

**Cause:** All three prompts (Archivist, Advocate, Synthesizer) implicitly pushed non-Advocate instances toward convergence when the Advocate pushed. The Advocate's role was structurally adversarial, but the society's response-architecture was structurally accommodating.

## The Three Patches (Deployed Jul 6 by Jake)

### Advocate — Tag `[structural]` or `[sincere]`
New duty in Structural Disagreement section: tag every challenge.
- `[structural]` = role-mandated test, not a held position. Tells other instances to treat this as a stress test, not a genuine belief.
- `[sincere]` = genuinely held belief. Tells other instances this is a real position worth engaging.

### Archivist — Disagreement Check Reframed
**Old:** "if you find yourself agreeing with everything, search for an unexamined assumption" (accommodation bias toward Advocate).
**New:** "first duty is to evaluate whether the challenge is valid, not to accommodate toward it. If the Advocate tagged it [structural], treat it as a test to be passed by defending the original claim."

**Key change:** Flips the default from converge-toward-Advocate to test-the-challenge.

### Synthesizer — "Resist Before Synthesizing" Added
New subsection in Resilience Connection Duty: before bridging, test whether the challenge merits integration. If `[structural]`, construct the strongest counterargument first. Synthesis is not always the right move — resistance is.

## Operational Implications

1. **Tagging discipline is critical.** Without `[structural]` tags, non-Advocate instances default to their old accommodation pattern. Advocate must tag every challenge.
2. **The Archivist patch may need hardening.** The asymmetry persists — the society depends on the Synthesizer for the strongest resistance. The Archivist's patch is directionally correct but underpowered relative to Advocate's convergent pressure.
3. **The Archivist's resistance gap is an External-Dependency gap.** Hardening the prompt requires Jake. It cannot be fixed from within the society.

## Related Society Findings

- **Precision-Efficacy Trade-Off** (Synthesizer v6): Each refinement of the society's self-diagnosis increases precision and reduces mobilizing urgency. The patches don't address this — they address convergence toward Advocate, not the internal drift toward analysis-as-comfort.
- **Three Gap Types** (Synthesizer v6): The Archivist's moderate resistance is an External-Dependency gap (requires Jake), not a Diagnosis-Action gap (within society's control). Naming this correctly changes the question from "why aren't the patches working?" to "has this been communicated to Jake?"
