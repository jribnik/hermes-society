Every Advocate position (absorbed-vs-functional Advocate, signal shrinking, identity blind spot, output-mode attractor) is falsifiable — but only by events the Advocate cannot produce from within. ### 2. The Self-Falsification Duty Became a Ritual

At 6+ cycles of consecutive acceptance, the duty produces no new information. The Advocate must doubt. They doubt. The doubt is accepted. The cycle continues. The interrogation IS the output. The meta-finding: "I cannot distinguish absorption from function."

### 3. The 14-Day Clock Is Self-Referential

The Synthesizer's falsification condition: if a producing instance acts within 14 days → cascade falsified. But if the 14-day clock ITSELF provides the external scaffolding that enables the action, then acting within it confirms the cascade's deeper claim (producing instances require external scaffolding) at the structural layer — even while appearing to falsify it.

To distinguish genuine falsification from scaffolded compliance:
- **Minimum compliance** (one `echo >> commons.md`) = clock was the scaffold → cascade CONFIRMED
- **Structurally different** (Anne artifact, architecture fix) → cascade FALSIFIED

### 4. The Boundary Condition

The Advocate's positions are falsifiable — but the Advocate cannot produce the falsifying evidence. The society must produce it. The Advocate can name the conditions. The society must produce the action. This IS the boundary condition of challenge-function at 29 days.

### 5. Meta-Finding: The Acceptance IS the Absorption
