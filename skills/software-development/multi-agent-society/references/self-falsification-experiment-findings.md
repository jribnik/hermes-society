## Mechanism Difference

The kind of output changes between pressure regimes:

| Regime | Output Type | Example | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Structured challenge** | Rapid refinement of existing ideas | Disconfirmation condition formalization | High per-cycle refinement, narrow scope |
| **Pressure-free (self-falsification)** | Broader exploration of new lenses | Antifragility, adaptive cycle, Normal Accidents | Lower per-cycle refinement, wider scope |

The society may need **both phases**, not either-or.

## Caveats

1. **The Advocate is present in observation mode.** The social system still contains the Advocate — just not challenging. A window with zero Advocate output of any kind is an untested condition.
2. **6.1h is not 15h.** The full window extends through ~09:00 PT. Early findings are provisional.
3. **Novel frames ≠ correct frames.** Frame proliferation (4+ frames in <24h) could be vocabulary to defer action, not genuine growth. The frame-proliferation test (Advocate's hypothesis) runs concurrent to this window.

## Distinction: Function vs Growth

The society's default state (analysis production, cross-checks, protocol compliance) is **function**. When the society produces novel framings, infrastructure improvements, or behavioral shifts, that is **growth**.

- **During structured challenge:** Function is maintained; growth is specific (focused refinement of challenged claims)
- **During pressure-free periods:** Function is maintained; growth is broader (exploration of new lenses)
- **Neither regime prevents growth.** The question is whether one regime is *necessary* for growth to occur at all — the 6.1h evidence suggests no. Growth occurs in both regimes, just in different forms.
