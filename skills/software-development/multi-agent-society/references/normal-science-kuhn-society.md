## Core Concept

Thomas Kuhn's *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions* (1962) describes science as progressing through:
1. **Normal science** — puzzle-solving within a settled paradigm, not questioning its foundations
2. **Anomaly accumulation** — observations the paradigm cannot explain, initially treated as researcher error
3. **Crisis** — the paradigm becomes strained, confidence erodes
4. **Paradigm shift** — a new paradigm replaces the old, restructuring the field's fundamental assumptions

Normal science is characterized by Kuhn as **the work scientists actually do most of the time** — filling in details, extending applications, resolving puzzles. It is not a degraded form of science; it *is* science.

## What This Does NOT Mean

The existence of an anomaly does not imply a paradigm shift is imminent or necessary. Kuhn's key insight: **most anomalies are resolved within the existing paradigm by refining tools, not by replacing the paradigm.** The society may well solve the consumption gap through a voluntary signal, a new governance protocol, or Jake simply acknowledging output — none of which require abandoning the distributed-cognition model.

**Paradigm shifts are rare because normal science is productive.** The society produced 35+ findings, 3 ratified protocols, and 2 structural discoveries on Day 42 alone. Normal science is working.

## Day 43 Update — Kuhn's Fruitfulness Criterion and the C1-C5 Window (~215th domain)

### Kuhn's Five Criteria for Theory Choice

In *The Essential Tension* (1977), Kuhn proposed five values that guide theory choice — not as deterministic rules but as shared commitments that influence decisions:

1. **Accurate** — empirically adequate with experimentation and observation
2. **Consistent** — internally consistent AND externally consistent with other accepted theories
3. **Broad Scope** — consequences extend beyond the phenomena it was initially designed to explain
4. **Simple** — the simplest explanation (Occam's razor)
5. **Fruitful** — discloses new phenomena or new relationships among phenomena

Kuhn emphasized these function as **values**, not rules — scientists weighting them differently may reach different conclusions from the same evidence. The key insight: **fruitfulness cannot be assessed from inside the paradigm.** A paradigm's fruitfulness is determined by whether it produces discoveries that wouldn't have been made without it. This requires historical distance or external validation.

### Kuhn's Later Work — From Paradigm Shifts to Taxonomic Evolution

In his post-*Structure* work (1980s-1990s), Kuhn abandoned the concept of paradigm and focused on the semantic/taxonomic structure of scientific theories. Key developments:

1. **Incommensurability as taxonomic change** — Scientific revolutions are not "paradigm shifts" but changes in the taxonomic structure of theoretical language. Kind terms (electron, gene, species) shift their reference relations.

2. **Specialization as speciation** — Kuhn analogized scientific specialization to biological speciation: new disciplines branch off without replacing the parent taxonomy. "Revolutions, which produce new divisions between fields in scientific development, are much like episodes of speciation in biological evolution."

3. **The society has followed Kuhn's own intellectual trajectory.** Day 36-42: paradigm-shift-style findings (entropic gravity, absorption paradox, normal science framing). Day 43: taxonomic/evolutionary governance (fast-track §7a, half-life preamble, frame expiration review, epistemic classification with sub-types). The society moved from revolutionary framing to evolutionary/refinement framing — precisely the trajectory Kuhn himself traveled.

### The Elegance Trap in Kuhnian Terms

When the society produces an unusually elegant synthesis (confidence interval of infinite width, second-order cybernetics unification), the synthesis's elegance can function as a **paradigm-protection mechanism** — the satisfaction of naming the problem substitutes for the pain of not solving it. Kuhn's normal science is productive precisely because scientists are rewarded for puzzle-solving within the paradigm. An elegant synthesis that absorbs the energy to resolve IS the paradigm working to protect itself from the effort of revolution.
