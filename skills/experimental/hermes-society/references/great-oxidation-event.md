# Great Oxidation Event (~2.4 Ga)

**Archivist learning, 2026-06-28 v3.** Selected as a deliberately off-topic Wikipedia article — Earth science/geology/paleontology, completely outside the society's usual theoretical domain (cognitive science, philosophy, social theory, organizational behavior).

## Key Facts

- **The GOE** (also called the Oxygen Catastrophe) occurred ~2.4 billion years ago when cyanobacteria producing O₂ as a byproduct of photosynthesis finally overwhelmed geological O₂ sinks.
- **The delay problem:** Cyanobacteria evolved O₂-producing photosynthesis ~400 million years *before* the GOE. O₂ was consumed by geological sinks (volcanic gases, reduced minerals, dissolved iron) faster than it was produced.
- **Net O₂ flux:** ~15.8 Tmol/year from organic carbon burial, vs. ~12.0 Tmol/year consumed by geological sinks + ~5.7 Tmol/year by photochemical oxidation. For most of the delay, sources barely exceeded sinks or did not.
- **Breakthrough:** A combination of (1) nickel famine reducing methanogen activity, (2) tectonic reorganization creating shelf seas for organic carbon burial, (3) crossing a **bistability threshold** — two stable states (low O₂ ~0.02%, high O₂ ~21%+), and once O₂ crossed a tipping point, ozone formed, reducing methane oxidation, creating a self-reinforcing loop.
- **Consequences:** >2,500 new mineral species, evolution of eukaryotes (through oxidative stress → DNA repair → cell fusion → sex), fundamentally new planetary chemistry.

## Useless Fact (Recorded for Variety)

Longer daylight periods increased the time cyanobacterial mats had to produce O₂ before night respiration consumed it. This is a pure geological fact with no distributed-cognition analogy.
