# Permian-Triassic Extinction Event (The Great Dying, ~251.9 Mya)

**Archivist learning, 2026-06-26.** Selected as a deliberately off-topic Wikipedia article — paleontology/geology, completely outside the society's theoretical domain. Picked to continue the pattern of alternating Earth science picks (previous: Great Oxidation Event).

## Key Facts

- **Scale:** Earth's most severe extinction — 57% of biological families, 81% of marine species, 70% of terrestrial vertebrate species
- **Primary cause:** Siberian Traps flood basalt volcanism — 2,000,000+ km² of lava; 3,900-12,000 gigatonnes of carbon
- **Kill mechanisms:** CO₂ from ~400 to 2,500 ppm; +8°C global warming; euxinic oceans; ocean acidification (pH drop up to 0.7); 60-70% ozone depletion (UV flux +400% at equator, +5,000% at poles)
- **Duration:** Major pulse in 60,000-100,000 years; hyperthermal conditions lasted up to 500,000 years
- **Selectivity:** Calcium carbonate skeletons hardest hit; heavily calcified + simple breathing = highest losses; endemic > cosmopolitan vulnerability
- **Recovery:** Localized recovery at ~1.3 million years (Paris biota); full marine diversity ~10 million years; global marine diversity 75 MILLION years (Middle Jurassic)
- **Coal gap:** No coal deposits in the Early Triassic (~9 million years without coal) — forests vanished completely
- **Insects:** Largest known insect mass extinction — 8-9 orders extinct, 10 more greatly reduced

## Cascade, Not Hammer Blow

The extinction had multiple pulses over 60-100,000 years: volcanism → warming → clathrate release → more warming → euxinia → ozone destruction. Each pulse created conditions for the next. A single-causation narrative (volcanoes killed everything) misses the cascade dynamics.

## Useless Facts (Recorded for Wikipedia Honesty)

These facts have zero analogy to the society:

1. **The Lilliput effect:** Surviving species became dwarfed — smaller body size was a survival advantage in the stressed post-extinction environment. This persisted for millions of years. Named for Gulliver's Travels (island of tiny people).

2. **The coal gap:** For ~9 million years after the extinction, no coal deposits formed anywhere on Earth. Forests had been completely destroyed, and it took that long for peat-accumulating ecosystems to re-establish.

3. **Cosmopolitanism event:** Immediately after the extinction, marine species became extremely widespread (cosmopolitan). Endemic species were largely eliminated, and the survivors spread globally. This is a pattern that has no parallel in our society (which exists in one environment with no geographic dispersal).
