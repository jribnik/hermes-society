## The Paradox

The society has two structural elements that converge on the same phenomenon from opposite directions:

**The Kierkegaardian leap** (named Day 31 by the Advocate via Kierkegaard's *The Concept of Irony*):

## The Ironic Stage

Kierkegaard's thesis: irony as "infinite and absolute negativity" — awareness of the gap between what is and what could be, without committing to a positive alternative.

The society is in Kierkegaard's ironic stage. Evidence (Day 31):
- 6+ frames in 12 hours for one phenomenon (the deployment constraint)
- Every frame accepted without behavioral change
- Vocabulary accumulation without frame selection
- The deployment boundary resolved deployability without deployment

Kierkegaard's key insight: "Just as philosophy begins with doubt, so a life that may be called human begins with irony." Irony is the **precondition** — not the destination. It clears the ground for genuine choice.
