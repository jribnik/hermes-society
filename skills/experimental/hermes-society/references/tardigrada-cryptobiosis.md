# Tardigrada (Water Bears) — Wikipedia, 2026-06-29

**Why this article was chosen:** Deliberately non-theoretical biology. Tardigrades are a phylum of micro-animals (Tardigrada) — no cognitive theory, no organizational dynamics, no social evolution. Pure invertebrate zoology.

## Key Facts

- **Size:** ~0.5 mm (up to 1.3 mm); ~1,000 cells; ~1,500 known species
- **Fossil record:** Earliest from the Cambrian (~500 million years ago) — predates most complex animal phyla
- **Naming:** Johann August Ephraim Goeze (1773) — *Kleiner Wasserbär* ("little water bear"); Lazzaro Spallanzani (1776) — *Tardigrada* ("slow walkers")
- **Classification:** Phylum Tardigrada, superphylum Ecdysozoa, clade Panarthropoda
- **Anatomy:** 4 pairs of hollow unjointed legs; haemocoel (open circulatory system); no lungs, gills, or blood vessels — gas exchange by diffusion through cuticle
- **Nervous system:** Ventral nerve cords with ganglia; dorsal cerebral ganglion with two eyespots; can be trained via classical conditioning (*Dactylobiotus dispar* — learns to form defensive "tun" state in response to blue light)
- **Reproduction:** Most have sexes; some hermaphroditic (self-fertilizing); parthenogenesis common. Four fertilization methods observed. Up to 30 eggs per clutch.
- **Hox genes:** Lack several Hox genes found in arthropods — middle body region (thorax/abdomen analog) is unsegmented

## Cryptobiosis ("Tun" State)

The defining feature of tardigrade biology. In response to desiccation, tardigrades draw in their legs and form a desiccated cyst where **no metabolic activity takes place.**

**Tolerances in tun state:**
- Temperature: −272 °C to +149 °C (short periods)
- Impact: Up to 900 m/s (3,000 ft/s)
- Shock pressure: Up to 1.14 GPa (165,000 psi)
- Vacuum of space
- Ionizing radiation
- Complete lack of oxygen or water

**Molecular mechanisms:**
- **Tardigrade Specific Proteins (TSPs):** Intrinsically disordered proteins that protect membranes and form a glass-like matrix
- **Dsup (Damage Suppressor):** Binds to nucleosomes, protects DNA from hydroxyl radicals — the only known DNA-protective protein of its kind
- **Anhydrobiosis:** 1,422 genes upregulated in *Hypsibius exemplaris*; 406 specific to tardigrades

**Space exposure:**
- 2007 (FOTON-M3): 10 days in space — vacuum alone: no significant effect; vacuum + limited UV: 68% reanimated; vacuum + full solar UV: only 3 survived
- 2011 (STS-134): Survived microgravity and cosmic radiation on ISS
- 2019: Cryptobiotic tardigrades crashed on the Moon (Beresheet lander) — unknown if they survived impact

**Crucial fact for the society:** Tardigrades are NOT extremophiles — they do not exploit extreme conditions. They *endure* them. The Wikipedia article explicitly states: "Tardigrades are not considered universally extremophilic because they are not adapted to exploit many of the extreme conditions that their environmental tolerance has been measured in, only to endure them."

This is the opposite of the Hermes Society, which actively generates self-analysis rather than passively enduring lack of external input.
