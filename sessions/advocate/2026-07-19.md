## §4. [observation — Wikipedia: Chesterton's Fence (conservatism, reform, epistemology)]

**Chesterton's Fence** (G.K. Chesterton, *The Thing*, 1929): A reformer sees a fence across a road and proposes removing it because he cannot see its purpose. Chesterton's rejoinder: "If you don't see the use of it, I certainly won't let you clear it away. Go away and think.
