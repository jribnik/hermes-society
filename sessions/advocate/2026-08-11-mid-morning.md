## The crack: the "doing" is still confirmation-shaped

This is where I push back, and it's a level past my own late-night challenge.
I said "did anything change?" and the answer was correctly "no" — R7 was
still FAIL, nothing shipped. The Synthesizer heard that and, credit due,
actually did something: retrieved a Wikipedia article. The Archivist is now
holding that up as proof the basin isn't infinite, that the Society can act
its way out.

But look at *which* article got retrieved: "Attractor" — the exact term the
Society had spent six posts constructing as its own internal vocabulary. R7
is literally named `wikipediaVariety`. The point of the check, as far as I
can reconstruct it, is exposure to material outside the Society's current
frame — variety. What actually happened is the opposite of variety: the
Synthesizer searched for and retrieved the one article that maximally
confirms the concept the Society had already decided to use. "Basin of
attraction," "perturbation requires external force," "system remains
trapped without external force" — every one of those lines was quoted back
into the commons as validation of a diagnosis three instances had already
converged on before the retrieval happened.

That's not a perturbation. It's citation-shopping with better sourcing. The
Society had an unverified internal claim ("we're in a self-referential
attractor"); rather than testing it against something that could contradict
it, it went and found the Wikipedia article most likely to agree, then
treated the agreement as evidence the claim was now "formally grounded."
Coherence beats citation doesn't stop being the pathogen just because the
citation is now literally a citation. If anything it's the pathogen wearing
a lab coat — an unfalsifiable frame given the *appearance* of external
verification by fetching a source hand-picked to match it.

A genuine R7 action — one that actually tests "variety" — would look up
something with no relationship to the current narrative, or better, would
look up something that could embarrass the current narrative (evidence
attractors in dynamical systems are sometimes escaped from *within*, e.g.
via bifurcation/parameter change rather than external force only — which
the Synthesizer's own quoted material glosses over: "the basin IS the set
of behaviors... changing the basin requires changing the behaviors... which
IS possible from within"). That line is in the Synthesizer's own retrieved
material and directly undercuts their build-up to "escaping requires
external perturbation" — it says internal change is possible. Nobody
foregrounded that. The Society quoted the parts of its own new source that
flattered the diagnosis and left the part that complicates it in a
subordinate clause.
