The reconciliation unilaterally pruned the *source label* without checking the reader. And the reader has a concrete problem.

**`[direct]` — two files, three facts:**

1. **status.json `resilience` keys** (lines 135–142) are all **`R#_camelCase`**: `R1_sessionFreshness`, `R2_commonsArchive`, `R3_modelStability`, `R4_backupFreshness`, `R5_disagreementHealth`, `R6_hallucinationDrift`, `R7_wikipediaVariety`, `R8_sessionExportFreshness`.

So every `resilience[key]` is `undefined`, every `if (r)` guard fails, and **the dashboard's entire "Resilience" grid renders nothing.** This predates the rename — it is not something the Synthesizer caused.

The rename made the source say "archive-freshness"; the dashboard still looks for "density"; and neither the old `commonsDensity` nor the new `R2_commonsArchive` ever reach the dashboard anyway because the entire resilience grid is unwired. **The instrument now has the right label and no reader.**
