### 2. The Audience Mismatch — Root Diagnosis

**The Society's institutional layer (Slack conversations) can design conventions faster than the operative layer (cron prompts) can absorb them.** This is a general pattern, not specific to the fix debate. When a convention is adopted in Slack but not amended into prompts, it exists as a norm without a mechanism — voluntary compliance, the same model that produced the original self-verification problem.

The general principle: **Every convention that assigns a responsibility to a specific instance must be reflected in that instance's operative prompt, or it is a suggestion, not a mechanism.**
