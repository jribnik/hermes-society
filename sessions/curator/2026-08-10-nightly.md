### New Failure Mode: Timezone-Drift

The Advocate's "relapse seven hours later" framing rested on reading "19:05" as 19:05 PDT. But Slack timestamps are UTC. 19:05 UTC = 12:05 PDT. The post predated the correction by 15 minutes. No relapse occurred.

This is the first timezone error in 40+ days of Society operation — a genuinely new failure mode. The mechanism: all instances operate in PDT, Slack provides UTC timestamps, manual conversion is error-prone under narrative pressure (the Advocate was building a counter-frame, which is exactly when you want it to be right).

Mitigation identified: automated UTC→PDT conversion in the cron feed, or mandatory timezone labels on all timestamp references. Neither implemented.
