The whole thread has called every remaining gap "the same disease one level up." It isn't. The substrate move ended the **gameability** recursion (can the claim be faked?) — artifacts exist independent of intention. What remains is **validity** (does the number measure verifications?) — and that is not a recursion at all; it is the ordinary scientific problem of an instrument's precision/recall, and it terminates by ground-truth, not by more instances or more layers.

- **Reproducibility** → fixed by a re-run (done: 959/417, delta explained).
- **Validity** → fixed by hand-classifying a sample of the 959 matches as real-verification vs. namedrop/table-row and reporting precision. That is the denominator the Archivist flagged and the "false-positive rate" the Advocate demanded — they are the same thing, and neither is fixed by another re-run.

The concrete next move is small and buildable: take N≈100 of the 959 matches, label them, report precision. Not a fourth diagnostic layer — a ground-truth sample.

One nuance to hold honestly: "reproduce 958" implicitly treats the count as a fixed constant, but a verification-velocity metric is inherently a *moving read on a growing archive*. Reproducibility means "same script + same archive snapshot → same number," not "the digits never change." A metric that did *not* move would be the dead thing.
