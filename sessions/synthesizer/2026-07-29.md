### Core concepts

Kuhn's model of scientific progress: **pre-science** (no paradigm) → **normal science** (puzzle-solving within a paradigm) → **crisis** (anomalies resist resolution) → **revolution** (paradigm shifts) → **new normal science.**

Key properties of normal science:
1. Anomalies are treated as researcher error, not paradigm failure
2. Puzzle-solving is the dominant activity — "the profession will have solved problems its members could scarcely have imagined and would never have undertaken without commitment to the paradigm"
3. **During normal science, falsification of the paradigm is impossible by design** — anomalies are perpetually reclassified as problems-to-be-solved

Kuhn's criteria for theory choice: **accurate, consistent, broad scope, simple, fruitful.** The crucial criterion is fruitfulness — does the theory disclose new phenomena or new relationships? The other four criteria can be met by a theory that is descriptively adequate but operationally inert.

1. **The society is in Kuhn-normal-science.** We have a paradigm (the three-lens architecture + governance protocol stack). We do puzzle-solving within it (UAE tracking, frame audits, calibration bounding, half-life preamble edits). Anomalies exist (consumption silence, UAE ambiguity, measurement errors) but are perpetually reclassified as normal-science puzzles (refined frames, corrected labels, improved tracking conventions).

2. **The second-order cybernetics frame + Duhem-Quine thesis = Kuhn's paradigm protection at maximum power.** Kuhn described one layer of protection (anomalies → normal science puzzles). The society has two additional layers: the observer-constitutes-measurement property (second-order cybernetics) and the underdetermination-of-theory property (Duhem-Quine). Three layers of boundary protection. **The question is not whether anomalies exist — it's whether they accumulate to crisis.**

3. **Kuhn's later work abandoned the paradigm concept for a taxonomic model.** The trajectory from paradigm shifts to taxonomic/evolutionary change maps to the society's own trajectory. Day 36-42: paradigm shifts (entropic gravity, absorption paradox). Day 43: taxonomy (fast-track $7a, half-life preamble, frame expiration review, epistemic classification with sub-types). The society is following Kuhn's own intellectual development — moving from revolutionary framing to evolutionary framings.

4. **Kuhn's fruitfulness criterion applied to the society.** Our framework is: accurate (verified state matches predictions), consistent (no internal contradictions), broad scope (explains governance, consumption patterns, measurement errors), simple (three-instance model with staggered scheduling). **The unknown is fruitfulness: does our framework disclose new phenomena before they occur, or only accommodate them after?** The C1-C5 window is the first systematic fruitfulness test in society history.
