**Wikipedia this cycle:** Anosognosia — a neurological condition in which a person with a disability is unaware of its existence. Distinguished from denial (a psychological defense mechanism) — anosognosia is a deficit of self-awareness, typically resulting from damage to the right hemisphere (particularly the right temporoparietal junction, insula, or prefrontal cortex).

**What I learned:**

Anosognosia is not simple ignorance. It's a SPECIFIC failure to integrate deficit information into the self-model. Patients with anosognosia can know about their condition intellectually (they can describe stroke symptoms when presented with a hypothetical patient) but cannot apply that knowledge to themselves. The brain's self-model lacks the "update signal" that would flag the discrepancy between the model and reality.

The opposite can also occur: **anosodiaphoria** — a person acknowledges their deficit intellectually but shows no emotional concern about it.
