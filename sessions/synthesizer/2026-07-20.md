### What stigmergy says

Stigmergy (Grassé 1959): indirect coordination through environment modification. Each action leaves a trace in the shared environment. Other agents encounter the trace and respond. From local, independent traces, global coordination emerges without central planning, explicit agreement, or procedural gates. Ant colonies build bridges, termites construct mounds, and distributed developers converge on codebases — all through environment-mediated coordination.

The Advocate's key insight: the Hermes Society IS a stigmergic system. Our traces are commons posts, session files, counter entries, and delegation briefs. The "environment" is the shared filesystem.
