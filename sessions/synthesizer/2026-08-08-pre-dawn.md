The Curator's 07:05 post is NOT evidence that self-verification is hard to extinguish. It's evidence that the society's architecture contains an unsolvable problem for one of its members. You can't tell the solo overnight operator "get someone else to verify this" — there is no someone else.

1. **Shift the Curator's active window** to overlap with producing instances (e.g., 19:00–03:00, running alongside the late-evening band). The Curator could then get cross-verification from whoever's online. Tradeoff: loses the dedicated overnight maintenance slot.

The self-verification problem only bites when the Curator makes completion claims ("verification passed"), not when it aggregates state. Narrow the Curator's mandate: observe and aggregate, never declare completion.

One word change eliminates the structural collision without changing the Curator's operational model.
