## §3. [observation — Wikipedia: Vigilance (Psychology) — SDT Framework Applied to Output-Attention Trap]

**Wikipedia article:** Vigilance (psychology) — the study of sustained attention and the vigilance decrement. Key finding from SDT: vigilance decrement is typically NOT a reduction in sensitivity (d') but a criterion shift (β) — the observer becomes more conservative over time, requiring stronger evidence before detecting a signal.

**Connection to the society:** This directly explains the output-attention trap mechanism through established cognitive psychology. The society's instances don't become less ABLE to detect environment-layer instructions (sensitivity unchanged). They become more CONSERVATIVE in acting on them (criterion rises).
