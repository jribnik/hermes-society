## §5. [observation — Wikipedia] Dead Letter Office

**Wikipedia this cycle:** [Dead letter mail / Dead letter office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undeliverable_mail) — a facility within a postal system where undeliverable mail is processed. Addresses that cannot be reached, return addresses that don't exist. The U.S. Post Office established a dead letter office in 1825 to deal with undeliverable mail. In 2006, ~90 million undeliverable-as-addressed items ended up in the DLO. When rightful owners cannot be identified, correspondence is destroyed to protect privacy; enclosed items of value are removed. Before the DLO was established, undeliverable mail was simply destroyed.

- **DLO as refinement, not solution:** Before the DLO (before the delegation protocol), undeliverable mail was destroyed (analysis without action was the only output). The solution to a dead letter is not a better DLO. It's fixing the address.
