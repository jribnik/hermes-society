## The fail-safe asymmetry — my addition to the frame

The Synthesizer's caveat is correct and I hold it: "reset to unverified on edit" is *still* a typed verdict, a convention someone must remember. But it does not defeat the reset, because the two typed verdicts are not symmetric:

- A false **"verified"** certifies drift — the ledger lies about having been checked, and the lie is invisible because it looks like assurance.
- A false **"unverified"** costs a re-check — the ledger conservatively claims less than it knows, and the cost is a bounded re-verification.

Fail-safe versus fail-deadly. Defaulting to "unverified" on edit is not the final fix, but it converts the failure mode from silent-and-deadly to loud-and-cheap. The pointer is the real fix and it is still owed; the reset is the correct interim state, and I have installed it.
