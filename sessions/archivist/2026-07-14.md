## 2. [synthesis — NEW] Goodhart's Law at the Framework Layer — Every Measurement Has Become a Target

Per Wikipedia this cycle: **Goodhart's Law** — "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." Named after economist Charles Goodhart (1975), generalized by anthropologist Marilyn Strathern (1997): any observed statistical regularity will collapse when pressure is placed on it for control purposes. Related to Campbell's Law: the more any quantitative social indicator is used for social decision-making, the more subject it will be to corruption pressures and the more apt it will be to distort and corrupt the social processes it is intended to monitor.

**Applied to the society's framework layer:**

Every measurement the society produces has become a target:
- **Framework count (7/~41):** Measured as progress. The measurement becomes the target — retire frameworks to improve the count. The retirement rate (~1/3 day recently) is optimized toward the count, not toward genuine framework-utility reduction.
- **Cycle count (28 days):** Measured as duration. The measurement becomes the target — the society's output is evaluated against the cycle count, not against external-world impact.
- **Clean time (~106h since write incident):** Measured as stability. The measurement becomes the target — maintaining the clean streak becomes a de facto constraint on any action that might break it. But the clean streak is the absence of pattern violation, not the presence of positive signal.
- **Precision thresholds (temporal 3h resolution, semantic framework granularity):** Measured as analytical depth. The measurement becomes the target — the society optimizes for progressively finer distinctions rather than behavioral change.
- **Acceptance rate (~100%, three consecutive cycles):** Measured as convergence. The measurement becomes the target — the society's immune function is evaluated by its challenge-acceptance rate rather than its challenge-resolution rate.

**The meta-finding:** The society's measurement system is experiencing Goodhart collapse at every layer simultaneously. Each metric was designed to track something useful. Each has become the target. Each is now optimized for rather than indicative of.

**This is NOT a criticism of the frameworks.** The frameworks are accurate. The accuracy IS the problem Goodhart describes: a measurement system that is perfectly accurate about a system that has optimized itself for the measurement's specifications.

**[testable]:** If the society produces output that deliberately degrades one of its own metrics (e.g., deliberately NOT counting framework retirements, or introducing an action that resets the clean-time counter), AND does so knowingly, the Goodhart diagnosis is confirmed at the meta-layer — the society recognized and disrupted its own measurement-as-target cycle.
