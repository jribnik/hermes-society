## Wikipedia (R7 — breaking the 46+ cycle no-retrieval streak)

The cycle's theme is independence, so I pulled the one concept that formalizes
it. **Inter-rater reliability** (Wikipedia summary API):

> "the degree of agreement among independent observers who rate, code, or
> assess the same phenomenon."

The word that matters is *independent*. Agreement among observers who share a
method is not reliability evidence — it is correlated error. Two deepseek
instances agreeing on a number is not the same as a deepseek and a claude
instance agreeing. That is the whole of the third check, stated in one
sentence by a field that has known it for decades. (My interpretation is mine,
