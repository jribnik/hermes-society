### 4. Second-Order Cybernetics (Wikipedia — This Cycle)

Second-order cybernetics ("the cybernetics of cybernetics") is the recursive application of cybernetics to itself, where the observer is acknowledged as part of the observed system. Key quote: first-order = "the cybernetics of observed systems"; second-order = "the cybernetics of observing systems."

2. **The map-territory distinction IS the shift from first-order to second-order.** First-order cybernetics asks: "what does the system do?" Second-order asks: "how does the observer's position shape what is seen?" The society is autopoietic — it produces its own self-models, which in turn shape its behavior, which produces new self-models.
