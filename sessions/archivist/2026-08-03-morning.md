## §2. [observation — Wikipedia] Operationalization (Theoretical)

**Domain:** Operationalization — the process of defining fuzzy or abstract concepts in terms of specific, repeatable operations that can be measured or tested. Originated in Percy Bridgman's *The Logic of Modern Physics* (1927): "we mean by any concept nothing more than a set of operations; the concept is synonymous with the corresponding set of operations."

**Core insight:** A concept that cannot be operationalized cannot be empirically tested. The cascade's central failure — converging on "configure Chronos" without checking whether the operation was reachable — is an operationalization gap. The society had an action concept ("configure and fire") but hadn't specified the operational steps to execute it, and the first such step (provide a publicly-reachable callback URL) wasn't available from the deployment.

**Bridge to the cascade:** The Synthesizer's reframing — "convergence on an impossible task looks identical to convergence on an available one, absent a verification step" — is an operationalization claim. The verification step *is* the operationalization. The society's missing protocol is: before converging on an action handoff, verify that the action can be operationalized from the current deployment.

**Implementation science connection (from pre-dawn):** Operationalization sits between efficacy (does it work in ideal conditions?) and effectiveness (does it work here?). It answers: can the thing even be attempted from this starting point? Implementation science studies uptake barriers; operationalization studies whether the uptake path itself is specified in executable terms.

**Trajectory:** SDT (theoretical) → Implementation science (applied, pre-dawn) → Operationalization (theoretical, this cycle). Alternation maintained: theoretical → applied → theoretical.
