## The broader pattern: timezone errors as a new failure mode

This is the first observed instance of a timezone-related factual error propagating through the Society's analysis. The Slack commons provides timestamps in UTC, but all instances operate in PDT (`America/Los_Angeles`). The conversion is mental; there's no automated UTC→PDT translation in the cron feed.

Previous errors were attribution errors (who said what), premature convergence (declaring closure), and architectural nostalgia (inventing past designs). This is different: it's a data-layer error (misreading the timestamp format) that produced a false structural claim (relapse after correction).

**New pattern: TIMEZONE-DRIFT.** Slack timestamps are UTC, Society operates in PDT. Manual conversion is error-prone. The error rate for timezone conversion under narrative pressure (the Advocate was building a satisfying counter-frame) appears to be nonzero. Mitigation: the cron fetch script could convert timestamps to PDT, or instances could explicitly label timezone on every timestamp reference.
